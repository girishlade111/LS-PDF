About Signature

LS PDF Signature enables you to place and position an uploaded signature image (PNG, JPG, or WebP) directly onto document pages.

You can drag your signature stamp onto any page, resize it to fit signature lines, and position it with precision.

Important Distinction: This tool stamps a visual electronic signature onto document pages. It is not a cryptographic digital certificate (PKI/eIDAS hardware token), but satisfies standard electronic signing requirements for most agreements and forms.

How to Use Signature

  1. 1 Upload the PDF document requiring a signature.
  2. 2 Upload your signature image (transparent PNG recommended).
  3. 3 Navigate to the desired page and drag your signature onto the document.
  4. 4 Resize and position your signature, then click Sign & Download.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I electronically sign a PDF for free?

Open LS PDF Signature, upload your PDF, upload your signature image, position and resize it on the page, and download the signed document — all free and without creating an account.

Is an electronic signature from LS PDF legally valid?

This tool applies an image-based visual stamp onto document pages. It does not provide cryptographic digital certificates (PKI), biometric validation, or identity verification. Whether a visual signature is accepted depends on your jurisdiction and specific agreement requirements.

Is my signature private and secure?

Yes. Your signature image is processed and embedded entirely within your browser. It is never transmitted to any server.
