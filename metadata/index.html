About Metadata

PDF files frequently carry hidden tracking metadata such as computer usernames, author names, software versions, and creation timestamps.

LS PDF Metadata lets you inspect all standard document properties and modify them to reflect accurate, professional information.

The Deep Clean feature strips all identifying tags and creator stamps from the document dictionary for maximum personal and corporate privacy.

How to Use Metadata

  1. 1 Upload your PDF document to inspect its current metadata.
  2. 2 Review existing properties: Title, Author, Subject, Keywords, Creator, and Producer.
  3. 3 Edit individual fields or click Deep Clean to wipe all identifying metadata.
  4. 4 Click Save Metadata to download the sanitized PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I edit the metadata of a PDF?

Upload your PDF to LS PDF Metadata, edit fields like title, author, and subject, then save. The updated PDF downloads with your new metadata.

Why should I edit PDF metadata for privacy?

PDFs often contain hidden metadata like the author name, creation software, and timestamps. Removing or editing this protects your identity and prevents information leakage.

Can I remove all metadata from a PDF?

Yes. LS PDF offers a "Deep Clean" option that strips all identifying metadata from your PDF, including producer, creator, and XMP data.
