About Merge PDF

LS PDF Merge reads multiple PDF documents directly into your browser memory and combines them into a single coherent document using pdf-lib.

You can freely rearrange documents or individual pages with interactive drag-and-drop reordering, apply per-file rotations, and preserve existing vector text, form fields, and embedded annotations without re-compressing contents.

All processing runs entirely on your local machine using client-side JavaScript. No document bytes, file names, or metadata ever leave your computer or mobile device.

How to Use Merge PDF

  1. 1 Upload two or more PDF files by tapping Select PDF Files or dragging them into the browser window.
  2. 2 Drag and drop the file cards to arrange them in your preferred reading order.
  3. 3 Optionally rotate individual documents or unlock password-protected files if prompted.
  4. 4 Click Merge PDF to assemble the files locally, then save your merged document instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I merge PDF files online for free?

Upload your PDF files to LS PDF Merge tool, drag to reorder if needed, then click Merge. Your combined PDF downloads instantly — no account or payment required.

Are my files uploaded to a server when I merge PDFs?

No. LS PDF processes everything 100% in your browser using WebAssembly. Your files never leave your device and are never sent to any server.

Is there a limit to how many PDFs I can merge?

There is no artificial limit. You can merge as many PDFs as your browser can handle in memory.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

Yes. LS PDF will ask you for the password and unlock the file locally in your browser before merging.
